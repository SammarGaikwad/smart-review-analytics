# IPTM Unit I: Market & Demand Analysis

## 1. Market Overview
The necessity for a centralized, multi-domain Smart Review Analytics Platform arises from the increasing volume of unstructured digital customer feedback. Organizations spanning multiple sectors require rigorous, data-driven insights rather than relying on manual sentiment interpretation.

*(Note: This is a qualitative academic demand analysis. No fabricated market statistics are presented.)*

### Target Market & Use Cases
- **Hospitality & Hotels:** Monitoring aggregated feedback regarding room cleanliness, service speed, and booking satisfaction across varied locations.
- **E-commerce & Electronics:** Comparing defect rates, delivery speed sentiments, and product durability keywords natively without switching platforms.
- **Entertainment & Movies:** Extracting temporal network sentiment tracking audience reactions post-premiere dynamically over time.
- **Restaurants & Food Services:** Rapid A/B testing or survey tracking regarding menu overhauls.

## 2. Demand Drivers
The platform addresses fundamental operational needs:
- **Increasing Online Reviews:** Volume scaling necessitates programmatic extraction (TF-IDF, Topic Clustering) to locate actionable intelligence.
- **Need for Customer Feedback Analysis:** Beyond a simple 5-star rating, businesses demand nuanced contextual understanding (e.g., negative reviews masked behind neutral scoring).
- **Need for Sentiment Understanding:** Moving beyond lexicon matching to machine-learning predictive arrays (Naive Bayes/SVM) reduces false-positive contextual errors.
- **Need for Product/Service Comparison:** Cross-domain graph networks allow visual identification of connected service failures.
- **Need for Automated Reporting:** Exporting findings safely via RBAC ensures management loops remain intact.

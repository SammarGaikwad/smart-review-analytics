# IPTM Unit I: Financial Appraisal

*Disclaimer: This is a hypothetical academic business scenario. The revenue and benefit numbers calculated below are purely demonstrative for IPTM evaluation and do not represent actual enterprise financial data.*

## 1. Financial Assumptions
- **Initial Investment (CAPEX):** $46,290 (Derived from *Cost Estimation* breakdown).
- **Annual Operating Cost (OPEX):** $10,183 (Calculated as $6,943 Maintenance + $3,240 Recurring Infrastructure).
- **Hypothetical Annual Expected Benefit / Revenue:** $35,000.
  - *Explanation:* Assumes a mid-sized hospitality/retail franchise averts $15,000 in customer churn via predictive sentiment detection and saves $20,000 in manual analyst labor hours annually by centralizing NLP extraction.
- **Discount Rate ($r$):** 8% (Standard academic benchmark for tech project NPV calculation).
- **Project Lifespan:** 3 Years (Standard tech obsolescence/upgrade cycle).

---

## 2. Core Financial Metrics

**Net Annual Benefit**  
`Net Annual Benefit = Annual Expected Benefit - Annual Operating Cost`  
$35,000 - $10,183 = **$24,817**

---

### A. Return on Investment (ROI)
Calculated cumulatively over the 3-year projected lifespan.

`Total Benefit (3 Years) = 3 × $24,817 = $74,451`  
`Total Investment = $46,290`  
`Net Benefit = Total Benefit - Investment = $28,161`  

`ROI = (Net Benefit / Investment) × 100`  
`ROI = ($28,161 / $46,290) × 100`  
**ROI = 60.83%** (over 3 years)

---

### B. Payback Period
`Payback Period = Initial Investment / Net Annual Benefit`  
`Payback Period = $46,290 / $24,817`  
**Payback Period = 1.86 Years** (Approximately 22 months)

---

### C. Net Present Value (NPV)
`NPV = Σ [CF_t / (1+r)^t] - Initial Investment`

Where $CF_t$ = Net Annual Benefit ($24,817), $r$ = 0.08, $t$ = year (1 to 3).

- **Year 1 PV:** $24,817 / (1.08)^1 = $22,978.70
- **Year 2 PV:** $24,817 / (1.08)^2 = $21,276.58
- **Year 3 PV:** $24,817 / (1.08)^3 = $19,700.53

`Total Present Value of Cash Flows = $63,955.81`  
`NPV = $63,955.81 - $46,290`  
**NPV = $17,665.81**

---

## 3. Financial Appraisal Summary

| Metric | Result | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial Investment** | $46,290 | Hypothetical Dev + Infrastructure cost |
| **Net Annual Benefit** | $24,817 | Expected operational savings & churn reduction |
| **Payback Period** | 1.86 Years | Recovers initial investment quickly within tech lifecycle |
| **3-Year ROI** | 60.83% | Highly profitable over the projected lifespan |
| **NPV (at 8%)** | $17,665.81 | Positive NPV validates investment viability |

**Conclusion:** Based on the hypothetical financial metrics, the Smart Review Analytics Platform demonstrates strong economic viability, a rapid payback period under two years, and a positive NPV, justifying authorization to proceed.
